# Service boundaries
Logical services: API Gateway, Identity and Access, Customer and Vehicle, Repair Request, Dispatch and Mechanic, Diagnosis and Estimation, Repair Orchestration, Workshop Transfer, Inventory, SLA and Scheduling, Notification, Payment and Invoice, Quality and Warranty, Audit, Analytics.
Estimation owns estimates and approvals; Orchestration owns repair authorizations; Workshop Transfer owns transport authorization. These permissions are deliberately separate. Responsibilities per service are browsable in the in-app Architecture Explorer.
